APPLICATION PURPOSE

What PromptCard AI - Image to Prompt does

PRODUCT / 01

Visual references become usable AI prompts

PromptCard AI - Image to Prompt is a Chrome extension that converts images or screen regions you explicitly select into detailed, structured AI prompts. Analysis starts only when you request it and can use PromptCard's integrated service with account credits or a compatible AI API you configure.

GOOGLE SIGN-IN / 02

Why PromptCard offers Google Sign-In

Google Sign-In is used only to authenticate your PromptCard account and provide account features such as session access, integrated credits, prompt history, and billing status. It does not give PromptCard access to Gmail, Google Drive, contacts, or unrelated Google data.

Learn how account and selected visual data are handled in the Privacy Policy.
